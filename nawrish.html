Our Story

A taste of Somalia on Commercial Road

Nawrish has become a Whitechapel favourite for guests trying Somali food for the first time — known for portions built for sharing, a warm complimentary lentil soup and banana to start, and staff who take the time to walk you through the menu.

Every plate leans on the same principle: generous, flavourful, and unmistakably home-cooked — finished with a cup of freshly brewed spiced tea.
